# VoltCart Chatbot — 15 Customer Questions & Answers

**Topic:** Electronics E-Commerce Customer Support

## Q1. How can I track my order?
**Answer:** Once dispatched, you receive an SMS/Email/WhatsApp with a tracking link. Delhi/NCR: 1–2 days; Metro: 2–4 days; Rest of India: 4–7 business days. Check "My Orders" on site or share your Order ID for live status.

---

## Q2. What is VoltCart's return policy?
**Answer:** We offer a 7-Day Easy Return policy. Product must be unused, in original packaging with all accessories. Refund is processed within 24 hours–7 business days depending on payment method.

---

## Q3. How do I claim warranty on a defective product?
**Answer:** Email support@voltcart.in with Order ID, photos/video of the defect. All products carry 1-Year Brand Warranty. Our team responds within 24 hours; free pickup and repair within 7–10 business days.

---

## Q4. I received a damaged/dead-on-arrival product. What do I do?
**Answer:** Report within 48 hours with an unboxing video (continuous, unedited), 3 photos of damage, and Order ID. Send to support@voltcart.in. Verified cases get free replacement within 48 hours.

---

## Q5. What are the shipping charges?
**Answer:** FREE shipping on orders above ₹499. Below ₹499: ₹49 (Delhi NCR) to ₹149 (remote areas). Express delivery in Delhi NCR available for +₹99 extra.

---

## Q6. Can I cancel my order after placing it?
**Answer:** Yes, before dispatch — full refund within 24 hours. After dispatch, cancellation is not possible; use the return policy after delivery. Act quickly as we dispatch orders very fast!

---

## Q7. I paid but no order was created / money deducted but no confirmation?
**Answer:** This is a common bank delay. The amount is auto-refunded within 24 hours (UPI) or 3–5 business days (card). If not refunded after 5 days, email support@voltcart.in with transaction ID/UTR and screenshot.

---

## Q8. Is No-Cost EMI available?
**Answer:** Yes! No-Cost EMI on orders above ₹5,000 for 3/6/9/12/18/24 months. Supported banks: HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, Citibank. Select EMI at checkout.

---

## Q9. I received the wrong product. What should I do?
**Answer:** Do NOT use the wrong product. Take photos and email support@voltcart.in within 48 hours with Order ID. Correct product dispatched within 24 hours of wrong-item pickup. Full refund if correct item is unavailable.

**Answer:** Report within 48 hours with your unboxing video and photos. Email support@voltcart.in with "Missing Item — [Order ID]" as subject. Missing accessory shipped free of charge within 2 business days.
