## 3. WARRANTY POLICY

### 3.1 Standard Warranty
- All products carry **1-Year Brand Warranty** from date of purchase.
- VoltCart additionally provides **VoltCart Care** support for the warranty period.

### 3.2 What is Covered
- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failure (non-physical damage)
- Battery defects (capacity below 80% within 6 months)
- Display issues not caused by physical impact

### 3.3 What is NOT Covered
- Physical damage (cracks, dents, water damage)
- Damage due to unauthorized repair or modification
- Damage caused by power surges or accidents
- Cosmetic damage (scratches) after delivery

### 3.4 Warranty Claim Process
1. Email **support@voltcart.in** with subject: "Warranty Claim — [Order ID]"
2. Attach photos/video of the defect
3. Team responds within **24 hours**
4. Free pickup → Repair at authorized center → Return in **7–10 business days**

## 4. DAMAGED / DOA (DEAD ON ARRIVAL) POLICY

### 4.1 Reporting Window
- Must be reported within **48 hours** of delivery.

### 4.2 Mandatory Requirements
- **Continuous, unedited unboxing video** (from before box is opened)
- 3 clear photographs showing the damage
- Order ID and contact details

### 4.3 Resolution
- Verified DOA cases: **Free replacement within 48 hours**
- Missing accessories: **Free shipping of missing item within 2 business days**
